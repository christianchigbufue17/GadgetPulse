Returns Policy

At GadgetPulse, we want our customers to receive their products in good condition and as described.

Damaged or Faulty Products

If your product arrives damaged, defective, or significantly different from what you ordered, please contact us as soon as possible after delivery.

What We May Ask For

Please provide your order details and clear photos or videos showing the problem with the product.

Change of Mind

Returns for a change of mind may not be accepted where the product has been opened, used, damaged, or is no longer in its original condition.

Return Conditions

  • The product should be in its original condition.
  • Accessories should be included where applicable.
  • Original packaging should be retained where applicable.
  • Proof of purchase may be required.

Refunds and Replacements

Depending on the circumstances, an approved return may result in a replacement, repair, or refund.

Contact Us

For return or product issues, contact GadgetPulse through WhatsApp or email.
