Shipping Information

At GadgetPulse, we deliver gadgets to customers across Nigeria.

Delivery Areas

We currently deliver across Nigeria.

Delivery Time

Delivery times depend on your location, product availability, and the delivery method used.

  • Lagos: Usually 1–2 business days
  • Other locations: Usually 2–5 business days

Shipping Fees

Shipping fees depend on your location and package. The applicable delivery fee will be communicated before your order is confirmed.

Order Processing

Orders are processed after payment and confirmation of product availability.

Delivery Address

Please provide a correct delivery address and an active phone number when placing your order.

Need Help?

Contact GadgetPulse through WhatsApp if you have questions about your delivery.
